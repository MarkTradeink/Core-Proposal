Please find below our invitation to tender for the controls upgrade of the T2
baggage handling system. Your response must address every clause below and will

End customer: Regional Airport Authority
Site: Terminal 2, baggage hall level -1

Reference documents:
  - ITT-2027-0431 rev C, dated 12 January 2027 — Invitation to tender
  - DWG-BHS-T2-100 rev F — Baggage hall general arrangement
  - SPEC-CTL-0012 rev B — Control system technical specification

Current situation: the T2 baggage handling controls were commissioned in 2011 on
a control platform whose vendor withdrew support in 2025. Sortation accuracy has
degraded to 97.1% against a contractual 99.5%, and the mean time to repair has
doubled over two years because diagnostics are no longer available.

Tender requirements:
  - 3.1 The Contractor shall replace all sortation controllers with a currently
    supported platform, retaining the existing field wiring.
  - 3.2 The Contractor shall demonstrate 99.5% sortation accuracy over a 72-hour
    continuous acceptance test.
  - 3.4 The system shall remain operational throughout the works. No more than one
    sortation line may be out of service at any time.
  - 4.2 The Contractor shall supply a full FAT and SAT protocol for approval prior
    to any works on site.
  - 5.1 The Contractor shall provide a compliance matrix against this
    specification, clause by clause.
  - 6.3 The Contractor shall provide a complete bill of materials and a detailed
    programme.
  - 7.1 Cyber security shall comply with IEC 62443-3-3 SL2.
  - 8.2 A twelve-month warranty with a four-hour on-site response is required.

Technical requirements:
  - Sortation controllers — 24 ud — currently supported platform, retaining
    existing field wiring
  - Network switches, managed — 12 ud — IEC 62443 compliant, redundant ring
  - Operator workstations — 4 ud — with diagnostics and reporting
  - Spare parts package — 1 ud — to cover twelve months of operation

Scope: equipment supply, engineering, installation, commissioning, project
management, spare parts, training and warranty. Shipping to site is included.

Constraints: the terminal operates 05:00 to 01:00 daily. Work is restricted to the
night window and no more than one sortation line may be isolated at a time.

Risks we expect you to address: interfacing with the legacy check-in conveyors,
which are out of scope but must keep running throughout.
